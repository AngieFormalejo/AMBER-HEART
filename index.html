Our idea

Old bottles. New heart.

Amber Heart Furnitures turns recovered materials into furniture that works and looks good. Pieces of recovered colored glass bottles go into the tabletop, and katsa fabric covers the bean bag, so both materials get another purpose.

Founded in 2026, we make lower priced, environmentally friendly furniture for homes, classrooms and other spaces.

    Questions

    Good to know

    What is the KatGlass Lounge Set made of?

    A bean bag covered in katsa fabric and filled with fiber fill, and a wooden table with a built-in shelf. The tabletop is decorated with broken pieces of recovered colored glass set in epoxy resin.

    Is it eco-friendly?

    Yes. The glass comes from used colored soft-drink bottles and the bean bag uses katsa fabric and a recycled plastic sack layer, so materials that would be thrown away get another use.

    Is the glass safe to touch?

    The glass pieces are completely covered by a smooth layer of epoxy resin, so the top is a flat, finished surface.

    Will every table look the same?

    No. The pieces of glass are arranged by hand and our tables come in a variety of designs, so you can choose the one you like.

    Who is it for?

    Students and anyone who wants a comfortable place to rest, read or study, at home or in the classroom.

    How much is it?

    We make furniture at a lower price than most. Use the form above to tell us what you want and we will give you the current price.
