About the company

Warm, recovered, made to be used

Founded in 2026, Amber Heart Furniture is a furniture manufacturing company that makes lower priced, environmentally friendly and good looking furniture, including lounge sets and tables. It combines sustainability, modern design and everyday usefulness so customers can improve their spaces with practical pieces.

The company designs, manufactures and distributes furniture for homes, classrooms and other settings.

The name comes from amber, the golden-orange glow of fire. The warmth stands for the drive to repurpose discarded materials, so broken glass becomes the heart of a modern interior.

Vision

To become an eco-friendly furniture brand that offers quality furniture for better living spaces, with practical and comfortable pieces for homes and schools that support a sustainable environment.

Mission

To design affordable, efficient and sustainable furniture from recycled products such as katsa fabric and glass, including lounge furniture comfortable enough for students at home and in school.

Our first product

KatGlass Lounge Set

The KatGlass Lounge Set is a katsa fabric bean bag chair and a wooden table with a built-in shelf. The bean bag is filled with fiberfill, so it is soft for reading or resting. The table is decorative and practical: its top is set with broken glass pieces in resin, and the shelf below holds books that students can read for entertainment.

What goes into it

What makes KatGlass, KatGlass?

Two distinctive materials come together in one functional and eye-catching furniture set. Glass is one of the major causes of waste, and large volumes of it still end up in landfill. Textile production adds to solid waste as well, so both get a second use here.

Making the bean bag

  1. Cut the katsa fabric for the side boxing, top panel and bottom panel.
  2. Cut the plastic sack bag to the size of the bottom panel pattern.
  3. Sew the katsa bottom panel and the plastic sack bag together.
  4. Sew the fabric pieces together, leaving an opening for the fiber fill.
  5. Fill with fiber fill until the volume and softness feel right.
  6. Close the opening to keep the fill inside.

Making the table

  1. Measure and cut the wood for the table top and the base.
  2. Nail the wooden pieces together.
  3. Paint the assembled table.
  4. Clean the discarded glass bottles, then break them into shapes using thick fabric as cover and gloves for the hands.
  5. Arrange the glass pieces on the table top.
  6. Put a thick layer of masking tape around the top edge so the resin cannot leak.
  7. Pour epoxy resin over the glass until it is covered.
  8. Let it harden for the curing time of the resin used.

Where we stand

SWOT analysis

SStrengths

Company

  • Good location. Many potential customers pass by, which makes it a good place to attract them.

Product

  • Affordable. Glass tables paired with a soft, bean-shaped couch at a fair price.
  • Eco-friendly. Made with recyclable materials such as katsa and broken glass.
  • Aesthetic design. Tables come in a variety of designs so customers can choose their own.

WWeaknesses

Company

  • New business. Low sales, little brand recognition and few loyal customers so far.

Product

  • Long production. Customization can lengthen production time.

OOpportunities

Company

  • Growing eco-conscious market. More consumers favor eco-friendly products.
  • Market expansion. Room to grow into regions that prioritize eco-friendly products.

Product

  • Product expansion. The same sustainable design concept can carry a wider product line.

TThreats

Company

  • Strong competition. IKEA and Mandaue Foam have strong brands and lower production costs.

Product

  • Logistics. Glass and resin are fragile, and damage in delivery could hurt our reputation.
